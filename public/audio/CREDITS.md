# FitQuest music and sound credits

Third-party source recordings used here are **CC0**. Original license files are included in `licenses/`; filenames and SHA-256 hashes of the source recordings are in `manifest.json`. Original FitQuest battle jingles are listed separately below, with hashes of the generated WAV files in the manifest.

## Music — dancramp

**8 Bit Retro RPG Music Asset Pack CC0**
https://dancramp.itch.io/8-bit-retro-rpg-music-asset-pack

- Base: `2-HOMESTEAD.wav`
- Gym and workouts: `6-ON_AND_UP.wav`
- Slime and Owl battles: `9-ENCOUNTER.wav`
- Golem and Shellback battles: `12-DARKWOOD.wav`

The complete original stereo loops were gain-adjusted and encoded to MP3 with gapless metadata. The shortened audition excerpts are not used during gameplay.

## Effects and jingles — Kenney

- Interface Sounds: https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds
- Impact Sounds: https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
- Music Jingles (NES set): https://kenney.nl/assets/music-jingles

Selected recordings were gain-adjusted and converted to mono PCM WAV for browser compatibility. Attack, guard, hurt, rep feedback, level-up, completion and Ultimate cues reuse these recordings. Ultimate layers heavy and metallic impacts and pitches its repeated charge cue upward.

## Original battle result jingles

- Victory: an ascending C-major fanfare with a sustained major chord.
- Defeat: a descending C-minor phrase ending on a low minor chord.

Both are original synthesized FitQuest cues, generated by `scripts/generate-battle-jingles.mjs` as mono PCM WAV. They replace the former Kenney result jingles and require no additional samples or downloads.

No runtime downloads from third-party audio hosts are required. Playback uses locally served files and the player's existing saved sound preference.
