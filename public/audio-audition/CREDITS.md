# FitQuest audio audition credits

All included source recordings are CC0. Original license files are preserved in `licenses/`.

- Music: **dancramp — 8 Bit Retro RPG Music Asset Pack CC0**, https://dancramp.itch.io/8-bit-retro-rpg-music-asset-pack
- Interface: **Kenney — Interface Sounds**, https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds
- Combat: **Kenney — Impact Sounds**, https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
- Rewards: **Kenney — Music Jingles**, https://kenney.nl/assets/music-jingles

Music previews were excerpted to 16 seconds, gain-adjusted with peak headroom, faded in/out, and downmixed/resampled to mono 22.05kHz PCM WAV for a lightweight audition. Obtain the original stereo loops from the source pack before final integration. Sound effects are unmodified Ogg Vorbis recordings.

`manifest.json` records source filenames, original durations for music, preview durations, and SHA-256 hashes of the original recordings. Suggested game roles are inferred from track titles; they are not a claim that the assistant has listened to the recordings. Use the player to make the final aesthetic choice.
